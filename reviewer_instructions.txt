# Mozilla Reviewer Instructions

This extension relies on authenticated access to University of Michigan lecture videos (Canvas, Kaltura, LeeCap).

Because we cannot provide our university credentials to external reviewers, we have included an HTML fixture that perfectly replicates the DOM structure of a typical supported Kaltura player.

## How to Test

1. Unpack the extension into Firefox.
2. Open `dist/fixtures/kaltura.html` in a new tab.
3. Click the extension icon.
4. You will see that the extension successfully parses the "Invented Kaltura Player" page and offers to download the transcript and slide contexts.

The extension makes no external network requests other than fetching same-origin VTT files when required (as seen in the fixture).
