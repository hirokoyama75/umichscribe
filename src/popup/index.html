AI Study Guide

How to study missed lectures with AI
Don’t settle for generic 5-bullet summaries. Use this 2-step pipeline to extract missed course actions and get taught 1-on-1:
1 Export Lecture as PDF

Select AI Context & PDF. The PDF embeds full-resolution slide images alongside synchronized speech so AI vision models can read math, diagrams, and code.

2 Reconstruct the Lecture

Open the AI of your choice (e.g., Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude). Upload your PDF and paste the Step 1 Reconstructor Prompt.

What it produces:
  • Checklist of missed deadlines, homework & exam info
  • Self-contained, modular Tutoring Packets
3 Learn with Your 1-on-1 AI Tutor

Open a fresh AI chat. Paste the Step 2 Tutor Prompt, then append ONE packet from Step 2 at the bottom under PACKET TO TEACH:.

How the AI teaches:
  • Teaches concept-by-concept with interactive checkpoints
  • Never gives away answers or jumps ahead before you understand
  • Generates a mastery handoff note when you finish
